Privacy Policy

1. Responsible party

RAW Electronic Solutions is responsible for Cobrando no ZAP and for the data processing it carries out in the app, on this website, and when providing support. For privacy matters, write to rawelectronicsolutions@gmail.com.

2. Data and purposes

The app uses the financial records you enter, such as descriptions, amounts, people, installments, due dates, and payments, to organize accounts payable and receivable, calculate balances, and provide reminders.

Records for people may contain a name, phone number, notes, and a photo linked to your address book. You can add people manually or select contacts to import, with your permission.

Card identification is limited to a nickname, type, bill due date, and optional last four digits. Full card numbers, security codes, and banking passwords are not requested.

Language, theme, currency, reminder, and message template preferences, along with an optional PIX key, are used to personalize your experience. The PIX key is included in messages when you select that option. If you contact us by email, we use your address, message, and attachments to respond to your request.

3. Storage and deletion

Records and preferences are stored on your device and are not sent to a server operated by RAW Electronic Solutions. Depending on your Android and account settings, this data may be included in backups or transferred to another device.

You can correct or delete records in the app. To remove local data, you can clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall it. This does not delete messages already shared or backups, which must be managed through the system or provider settings.

Support data is retained for as long as necessary to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. Third-party services also follow their own retention periods and deletion controls.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google's advertising services. These services may collect and process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and performance information to display and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain security. The IP address may also indicate an approximate location.

The app does not provide financial records, contacts, card data, or PIX keys for ad targeting. See the Google Privacy Policy.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses and access logs for operational and security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Advertising, hosting, email, and backup services may process data in other countries in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

5. Sharing messages

When you choose to open or share a message in WhatsApp or another service, the phone number and prepared text may be passed to that service, including financial and PIX details you select. Sending requires action on your part; Cobrando no ZAP does not send messages automatically.

Shared content is then processed by the selected service and the recipient. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. When recording or sharing other people's data, respect their privacy and rights.

6. Permissions and choices

Access to your address book is optional and can be denied or revoked in your device settings. This does not delete contacts already imported. You can also disable notifications and adjust reminder options. Battery restrictions may affect these notifications.

Advertising choices are available in Android and Google controls and, where applicable, in the app's ad privacy options. Restricting personalized ads does not stop all processing of technical data. The app does not request precise location.

7. Rights and security

Data is processed to provide the requested features, respond to requests, meet legal obligations, and protect rights, with consent where necessary or on another basis permitted by law. Using the app does not constitute blanket consent for all purposes.

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), you may request access, correction, information about sharing, deletion, anonymization, blocking, and portability where applicable, as well as withdraw consent and object to unlawful processing. You may also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority.

Send your request to rawelectronicsolutions@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity to protect your data. We do not have remote access to local records, but we can provide guidance on correcting or deleting them.

We use app and system safeguards to protect data, without guaranteeing absolute security. The app is not directed at children. This policy may be updated; significant changes will be communicated, and new consent will be requested when necessary.
